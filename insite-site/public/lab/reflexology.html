Self-care

A five-minute foot ritual

Sit comfortably with one foot across your knee, a little lotion or oil on your hands. Press the button and we’ll guide you, one minute per step.

  1. Warm & open

    Hold the foot between both palms, then gently twist it like wringing a towel, heel to toes.

  2. Thumb-walk the spine

    Inch your thumb along the inner edge from heel to big toe in tiny caterpillar steps.

  3. Circle the toes

    Gently rotate and squeeze each toe, finishing with slow circles on the big-toe pad.

  4. Knead the arch

    With your knuckles, make slow, firm circles through the arch, easing off any tender spots.

  5. Settle the heel

    Cup and press the heel, then stroke from toes to ankle three times. Breathe out slowly with each stroke.
