Questions

Common questions

Why do supplements matter before surgery?

Some herbs and supplements can affect bleeding, blood pressure, blood sugar, or how anesthesia medications work. Anesthesia professionals commonly ask patients to disclose all of them — and often to pause certain ones ahead of surgery. Use the Surgery-Ready Checker to prepare your list, then confirm with your care team.

Should I stop my prescription medicines?

Never stop a prescribed medication on your own. Some should be continued and some adjusted — your surgeon, prescriber and anesthesia provider will tell you exactly what to do.

Can integrative care help me prepare or recover?

Many patients find breathing practices, relaxation, and good nutrition helpful for feeling calmer before a procedure and supporting recovery afterward. Ask about building a pre- and post-surgery plan with OPTIMIZE².
